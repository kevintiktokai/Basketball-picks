Best 5 a day, plus the parlay when there is one

Each day the locked engine bets its five strongest picks at 53% or better as straight bets. On days it can build one, it also plays the 2.5+ parlay: two legs from different games, combined odds of 2.5 or more. Every season starts from $5,000. The parlay counts as one bet with the same stake as a straight bet. Turn on doubling to see the same bets with 1×, 2×, 4× stakes after a loss.

What these numbers assume

  • Straight bets: before 2021-22, the consensus opening total at −110 (1.909). From 2021-22, the best opening line and price among the US books in the data, shown with the book.
  • The parlay's legs are often moved lines (a few points better than the opener). Their prices are our estimate of the book's price, not a real quote, so check the price on the day; the engine needs combined odds of 2.5 or more.
  • A parlay leg can be the same game as one of the day's straight bets. A pushed leg drops out and the parlay pays at the other leg's price.
  • The bets of a night are placed together: in the 2% view each stake is 2% of the balance at the start of the night.
  • Doubling: after a loss the next stake doubles, at most twice (1×, 2×, 4× the base), then resets after a win or a third loss. Straight bets and the parlay share one run: a losing parlay doubles the next bet, and a win of either kind resets it. "After each losing bet" goes in tip-off order (a parlay settles with its later leg) and assumes each result is known before the next bet, which busy nights do not allow. "After each losing night" doubles every bet of the next night after a night that lost money overall; that is the version you could follow. A night never stakes more than the balance.
  • The 2% view compounds within a season, so the best seasons end far above $5,000. Books cap stakes and limit winning accounts long before that.
  • 2021-26 was already used once to test the engine. This shows what following the plan would have looked like, not a promise about next season.
