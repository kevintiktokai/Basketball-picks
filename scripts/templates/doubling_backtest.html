Doubling after a loss: what it does to a season

Every straight bet the locked engine would have made over 15 seasons, replayed three ways from a fresh $5,000 each season: the same stake every bet, doubling after each losing bet, and doubling the whole next night after a losing night. Doubling goes 1×, 2×, 4× the base, then resets after a win or a third loss.

What these numbers assume

  • Doubling after each losing bet needs the last result before the next bet. NCAAB nights carry up to 75 bets, many tipping at the same time, so that version could not really be followed; it is shown in tip-off order. Doubling by night is the version you could follow.
  • A night's bets never stake more than the balance. After two losing nights the nightly plan stakes four times the base on every bet, which on a busy night can be most of the account.
  • Every season starts again from $5,000. Prices: standard −110 before 2021-22, each game's best real opening price after.
  • The percentage plans compound inside a season, which is why some seasons end above $100,000. Books cap stakes and limit winning accounts long before that.
  • Doubling does not change the return per dollar bet. It changes how much is bet and when, which raises both the good seasons and the risk of ruin.
