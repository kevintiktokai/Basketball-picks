How to read this

  • Pick is the line the bet was graded at. Before 2021-22 that is the consensus opening total at −110 (1.909). From 2021-22 it is the best opening line and price among the US books in the data (BetMGM, BetRivers, DraftKings, bet365, FanDuel, Caesars, Fanatics), shown with the book; Opener is the consensus opening total.
  • Engine is the locked engine's calibrated chance that the pick wins. Tiered stakes $100 at 55%+ and $50 at 53–55%.
  • With doubling on, a stake in orange is above its normal size (2× or 4× after a loss). Doubling after each losing bet assumes each result is known before the next bet, in tip-off order.
  • Balance is the account after the bet. With doubling after each losing night, every bet of a night is placed together, so the balance moves bet by bet within the night as the results come in.
  • A push returns the stake. Games with no clean opening total are not in the backtest.
  • 2021-26 was already used once to test the engine, and the 53% rules were chosen on 2011-21. This shows what following each rule would have looked like, not a promise about next season. The full ledger is also in the repo as reports/every_bet_2011_26.csv.
