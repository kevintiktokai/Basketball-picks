How to read this

  • Straight bets use the best opening number and price across books (BetMGM, DraftKings, Caesars, bet365, FanDuel, BetRivers), ignoring any book more than 3 points off the others. A price of 1.909 is −110.
  • The 2.5+ card's legs are mostly moved lines (alternate totals a few points better than the opener). Their prices are our estimate of the book's price, not a real quote, so check the price on the day.
  • Stakes are 1% of the morning balance, the same for every bet that day; the card counts as one bet. A push returns the stake.
  • In 2023-24 the engine's straight picks were mostly Overs (1,240 of 1,331). Teams play their first games before the engine has enough data, so betting starts in mid-November.
  • This replays a season that was already used to test the engine. It shows what following the plan would have looked like, not a promise about next season.

Source: locked v3 engine and the locked 2.5+ card rules; reports/ledger_2023_24.csv has every bet.
