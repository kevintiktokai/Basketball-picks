# European straight bets under four staking plans

50 backtest bets (26 won, average price 1.96, -0.9% per bet, +3.1% against Pinnacle's close), from a $5,000 account. 38 of the 49 later bets were placed before the previous game had finished, so bet-by-bet doubling could not be followed as replayed; night by night could.

| staking | doubles after | ending balance | profit | lowest | biggest drop | staked | return per $ staked | lost all three |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Flat $100 | never | 4,954.800 | -45.200 | 4,549.000 | 723.400 | 5,000.000 | -0.009 | 0 |
| 2% of account | never | 4,907.455 | -92.545 | 4,542.083 | 727.868 | 4,865.700 | -0.019 | 0 |
| Doubling $100-$400 | each losing bet | 5,421.900 | 421.900 | 4,164.300 | 1,440.000 | 8,300.000 | 0.051 | 3 |
| Doubling 2%-8% | each losing bet | 5,233.849 | 233.849 | 4,124.821 | 1,511.391 | 8,440.466 | 0.028 | 3 |
| Doubling $100-$400 | each losing night | 4,438.500 | -561.500 | 3,627.500 | 1,960.000 | 8,200.000 | -0.068 | 1 |
| Doubling 2%-8% | each losing night | 4,124.567 | -875.433 | 3,539.776 | 2,061.359 | 7,381.120 | -0.119 | 1 |
