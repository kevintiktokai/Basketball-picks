A season at $50 or $100 a bet

Every bet gets the same stake: each straight bet, each 2.5+ card, each main-line double. These are 20,000 simulated seasons replayed from the 2021–26 seasons at real opening prices, keeping their busy Saturdays, quiet weeks and lean years. Everything scales with the stake: at $100 every figure is double the $50 one.

Before you size up

  • Straight bets bunch up on big slates: on a busy Saturday the engine can produce 60 or more picks. At $100 each that is several thousand dollars in play in one day, spread across your books.
  • “Cash on hand” covers a 1-in-20 bad stretch plus the busiest day's stakes. It is the money to keep across your accounts so a cold run never stops you from placing the next bet.
  • Results assume each game's best opening price across books, bet soon after the line appears. Parlays sit at one book; at −110 there the cards earned about 5 points less per card.
  • $50–$100 is well inside normal limits for college totals and parlays, but books limit accounts that win steadily, so expect to need several books over a season.
  • Profit per season assumes next season behaves like one of 2021–26. 2024-25 was a near break-even year for the straight bets, and the losing-season odds include years like it.
