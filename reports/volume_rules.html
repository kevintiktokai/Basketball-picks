Every season

Period: build seasons were used to develop the engine, check seasons to confirm it (both at −110), and test seasons were scored once with it locked, at each game's best real price. These rules were chosen on 2011-21; 2021-26 is a re-check, so they still need to prove themselves live.

What these numbers assume

  • The engine is unchanged; only the rule for which picks to bet, and how much, differs.
  • Doubling: after a loss the next stake doubles, at most twice (1×, 2×, 4× the bet's own stake, so a half stake goes $50, $100, $200), then resets after a win or a third loss. "After each losing bet" assumes every result is known before the next bet, in tip-off order, which busy nights do not allow; "after each losing night" is the version you could follow.
  • Each season starts again from $5,000. In the 2% view, a half stake is 1%, each stake is a share of the balance at the start of the night, and a night never stakes more than the balance.
  • Lower-probability picks win less often: the 53–55% band wins about 55% of the time against about 57–58% for 55%+. They add profit because they are still above the break-even of about 52.4% at −110.
  • The 2% view compounds within a season, so the biggest seasons end far above $5,000. Books cap stakes and limit winning accounts long before that.
  • 2021-26 prices assume betting at the best of 6+ books soon after the opener appears. At −110 everywhere, every rule earns roughly 1.5 points less per bet.
