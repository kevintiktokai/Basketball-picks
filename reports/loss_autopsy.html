The home floor's park factor

Some floors keep producing totals above or below their closing lines, season after season. The reasons could be thin air in the Mountain West, rims and sight lines, or how a team plays at home. Team ratings average home and road games, and the opener is built from team numbers, so part of a floor's effect is left over. We scored each floor from its earlier home games only: the total minus the closing line, averaged over the last three seasons and shrunk toward zero (sum ÷ (games + 40)).
