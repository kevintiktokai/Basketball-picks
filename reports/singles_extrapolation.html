What these numbers assume

  • Only 2021–26 is a clean test: the engine was locked before those seasons were scored. 2011–18 built it and 2018–21 checked it, so those years flatter it somewhat.
  • 2021–26 prices are each game's best opening price across 6+ books. That means accounts at several books and betting soon after the opener appears; at −110 the same bets earn about 1.5 points less.
  • Volume swings: the engine bets only when its probability clears 55%, and that happened 88 times in 2025-26 and 1,380 times in 2021-22.
  • 1/8 Kelly stakes about 0.7% of the bankroll at 55% and about 2% at 62%; a day's stakes are capped at 25%.
  • Books cap stakes and limit accounts that keep winning, so the compounding figures are the upper end of what a real bankroll can reach.
